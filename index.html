▶Controls and two players

    
    
      
  • L + R + ZR opens the Switch controller screen: up to 4 controllers (Pro Controller, a pair of Joy-Con, or one Joy-Con per player).
    • 
      
  • A single Joy-Con held sideways works as a full controller: the stick moves, the four buttons under your right thumb are A/B/X/Y in the same places as on a Pro Controller, SL/SR are the triggers and − or + is Start. To pair it, hold it sideways and press SL + SR on the controller screen.
